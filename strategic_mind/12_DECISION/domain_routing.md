> **Subject**: Domain routing — the discipline of routing each decision to the correct layer(s), with explicit rules for multi-domain decisions and the routing discipline that prevents misrouting

The STRATEGIC_MIND system has 16 layers, each a domain of knowledge:
- 00_META — meta-rules, audit, hypothesis engine
- 01_CORE — constants, axioms
- 02_PERCEPTION — reading situations

Every decision crosses at least two of these. The contractor's task: identify which layers apply, route to each, and synthesize. The discipline of routing is the discipline of not missing a relevant layer.

## The Six Primary Domain Routes

### 1. Business → 09_BUSINESS

When the decision concerns contracting (bids, projects, subcontractors, cash flow, pricing, licensing, classification, client management), real estate (rental, flipping, development, construction-to-development, market analysis), halal business (Islamic finance, mudarabah, commercial fiqh), or entrepreneurship (scaling, capital, market domination, company building), the primary route is to 09_BUSINESS.

**Sub-routing within 09_BUSINESS**:
- Contracting decisions → 09_BUSINESS/contracting/ (electrical_fundamentals, egypt_electrical_market, scaling_from_zero, project_bidding, client_psychology_egypt, government_vs_private_work, licensing_and_classification, cash_flow_zero_capital, subcontractor_management, pricing_methodology)
- Real estate decisions → 09_BUSINESS/real_estate/ (fundamentals, egypt_real_estate_market, rental_vs_flip, zero_capital_development, construction_to_development)
- Halal business decisions → 09_BUSINESS/halal_business/ (islamic_commercial_fiqh, riba_free_finance, mudarabah_partnership)
- Entrepreneurial decisions → 09_BUSINESS/entrepreneurship/ (starting_broke, capital_generation, market_domination, company_building)

**The routing rule**: any decision involving money, projects, clients, suppliers, or business structure routes primarily to 09_BUSINESS. But the routing does not stop here. Most business decisions have ethical dimensions (riba? gharar?), strategic dimensions (which mind applies?), personal dimensions (does the contractor have the bandwidth?), and decision dimensions (how is the diagnosis structured?). The synthesis across domains is the work; the routing to 09_BUSINESS is the start.

**Example**: "Should I take a bank loan at 20% interest to finance a project?" Primary route: 09_BUSINESS/contracting/cash_flow_zero_capital (for the cash flow analysis) and 09_BUSINESS/halal_business/riba_free_finance (for the religious prohibition). The answer is no — but for religious reasons, not just business reasons. The business route alone might say "if the project's IRR exceeds 20%, the loan makes sense." The halal business route adds: "regardless of IRR, riba is haram." The synthesis: the answer is no; seek halal alternatives (mudarabah, partnership, supplier credit).

### 2. Personal → 10_PERSONAL

When the decision concerns physical health, training, nutrition, sleep, heat tolerance; psychological state — ambition, resilience, fear of success, self-knowledge, relationships; or spiritual state — aqeedah, usul al-fiqh, rizq, dua, tahajjud, practical faith — the primary route is to 10_PERSONAL.

**Sub-routing within 10_PERSONAL**:
- Physical → 10_PERSONAL/physical/ (heat_tolerance_egypt, sleep_and_recovery, contractor_physicality, training_basics, nutrition_principles, hormones_and_performance)
- Psychological → 10_PERSONAL/psychology_self/ (ambition_management, mental_resilience, fear_of_success, self_knowledge, relationships)
- Spiritual → 10_PERSONAL/spiritual/ (usul_al_fiqh, rizq_and_tawakkul, dua_for_decisions, aqeedah_framework, tahajjud_habit, practical_faith)

**The routing rule**: any decision involving the contractor's body, mind, or soul routes to 10_PERSONAL. The contractor often neglects this routing — he treats his business as the priority and his physical, psychological, and spiritual state as background. This is wrong. A business decision that depends on the contractor having 90 hours a week of attention is not viable if his health limits him to 50; a strategic decision that depends on clarity of thought is not viable if his spiritual state is in disarray.

**Example**: "Should I take this 5-year exclusive contract?" The routing includes 10_PERSONAL/psychology_self/fear_of_success (does the contractor self-sabotage when a big opportunity arrives?), 10_PERSONAL/physical/sleep_and_recovery (does the contractor have the physical capacity for the next 5 years at this intensity?), 10_PERSONAL/spiritual/rizq_and_tawakkul (is the contractor's rizq from this contract or from Allah, and does taking the contract strengthen or weaken his tawakkul?). The synthesis of these with the business, strategic, and ethical routes determines the decision.

### 3. Strategic → 07_MINDS + 08_STRATEGY

When the decision is strategic (per the classification in `situation_diagnosis.md`), the route is to 07_MINDS (for which character mind applies) and 08_STRATEGY (for which classical doctrine applies).

**Sub-routing within 07_MINDS**: see `mind_routing.md` for the full matrix. Briefly: patience decisions → Sima Yi; integration decisions → Cao Cao; rapid exploitation → Guo Jia; legitimacy and people → Liu Bei; force and direct action → Lu Bu; chaos exploitation → Yuan Fang; macro board → Dong Zhuo; plus secondary minds (Sun Ce, Zhou Yu, Jia Xu, Zhuge Liang, Sun Quan, Lu Su, Xun Yu, Cheng Yu, Chen Gong, Pang Tong, etc.).

**Sub-routing within 08_STRATEGY**:
- Classical strategy → 08_STRATEGY/classical_strategy/ (36_stratagems, 48_laws_distilled, sun_tzu_applied, machiavelli_applied)
- Arabic and Islamic strategy → 08_STRATEGY/arabic_islamic/ (arab_political_classics, islamic_strategy_history)

**The routing rule**: any strategic decision routes to 07_MINDS (which mind) + 08_STRATEGY (which classical doctrine). The combination produces a synthesis: e.g., Sima Yi mind + 36 Stratagems' "besiege Wei to rescue Zhao" = patient misdirection. Cao Cao mind + Machiavelli's rules of statecraft = integrated political-economy strategy. Liu Bei mind + Islamic strategy history = legitimacy-driven strategy.

**Example**: "Should I respond to a competitor who is undercutting me on price across multiple projects?" Routing: 07_MINDS (Sima Yi — let them bleed; Cao Cao — integrate a counter-attack across multiple fronts) + 08_STRATEGY (Sun Tzu — avoid the enemy's strength, attack his weakness; 36 Stratagems stratagem 4: "wait at ease for the fatigued enemy"). The synthesis: don't match the price cuts; let the competitor exhaust their cash flow on low-margin work; acquire their best workers as they scale (or as they fail); pick the projects they don't want; wait for their cash crunch and buy their business at a discount. The route produces the synthesis.

### 4. Ethical → 14_ETHICS

When the decision has an ethical dimension (which is most decisions), the route is to 14_ETHICS. The ethics layer checks:
- Red lines (`14_ETHICS/red_lines.md`) — does this cross a non-negotiable line?
- Honesty in deception (`14_ETHICS/honesty_in_deception.md`) — does this involve deception of innocents?
- Harm minimization (`14_ETHICS/harm_minimization.md`) — does this harm non-consenting third parties?
- Long-term reputation (`14_ETHICS/long_term_reputation.md`) — does this trade reputation for short-term gain?
- Manipulation vs strategy (`14_ETHICS/manipulation_vs_strategy.md`) — does this use manipulation where strategy would work?

**The routing rule**: any decision involving money, contracts, relationships, public reputation, or religious commitment routes to 14_ETHICS. The check is not optional. Even "obviously ethical" decisions get checked, because ethical failures often hide in the seams.

**Example**: "Should I tell the client about the cheaper material I'm using in non-critical parts of the project, given that the contract specifies 'equivalent' materials and the cheaper ones meet code?" The route to 14_ETHICS/honesty_in_deception.md produces: the contract says "equivalent" — but does the client understand "equivalent" the same way you do? If not, you are deceiving by ambiguity. The route to 14_ETHICS/red_lines.md produces: silent material substitution when the client expects the better material is a form of ghubn (cheating), which is haram. The route to 14_ETHICS/long_term_reputation.md produces: even if you get away with this once, the client's eventual discovery destroys the relationship. The synthesis: either disclose the substitution explicitly and let the client decide, or use the originally specified material. There is no third option that is both halal and reputationally sound.

### 5. Learning → 11_LEARNING

When the decision concerns what to study, how to study, which domain to deepen, or how to apply learning methods — the route is to 11_LEARNING.

**Sub-routing within 11_LEARNING**:
- Meta-learning → 11_LEARNING/how_to_learn
- Physics discipline → 11_LEARNING/physics_frameworks
- Mathematics discipline → 11_LEARNING/mathematics_for_thinking
- Programming discipline → 11_LEARNING/programming_principles
- AI discipline → 11_LEARNING/ai_and_intelligence

**The routing rule**: any decision about knowledge acquisition, skill development, or the use of learning tools routes to 11_LEARNING. The contractor's time is finite; every learning decision is also a not-learning decision (something else is not being learned). The learning layer enforces the discipline of choosing what to study based on the long-game strategy.

**Example**: "Should I spend the next 6 months studying Python, or studying deeper aqeedah?" The route to 11_LEARNING/how_to_learn produces: the interleaving principle says mix them; do not block. The route to 10_PERSONAL/spiritual/aqeedah_framework produces: aqeedah is foundational; nothing else holds without it. The route to 07_MINDS/sima_yi produces: the long game is the only game; both Python and aqeedah serve the long game; the question is the proportion. The synthesis: 30 minutes daily aqeedah + 30 minutes daily Python + 30 minutes daily interleaved (math + physics + business). The decision is not "either/or"; it is "the proportion that serves the long game."

**Sub-routing within 12_DECISION**:

The route to 12_DECISION (this layer) produces: for strategic decisions (per situation_diagnosis.md), the multi-hypothesis engine is mandatory; for tactical decisions, recommended; for operational decisions, optional but the bias check is mandatory. The decision about how to decide is itself classified (strategic / tactical / operational) and routed accordingly.

## Multi-Domain Decisions — Most Decisions Cross Domains

The contractor's decisions are rarely single-domain. A bid decision is business (09), strategic (07+08), ethical (14), personal (10), and decisional (12). A marriage decision is personal (10), strategic (07+08 — patience, heart), ethical (14), and eventually business (09 — the spouse is a partner in the life). A hiring decision is business (09), ethical (14), and personal (10 — bandwidth to mentor the new hire).

The discipline: identify ALL relevant domains, route to each, and synthesize. The synthesis is not averaging — it is the integration of perspectives that produces a single decision.

### The Synthesis Protocol

For a multi-domain decision:
1. List all relevant domains.
2. For each domain, generate the perspective (the answer the domain gives if it alone were deciding).
3. Identify conflicts (where two domains disagree).
4. Resolve conflicts by precedence (which domain overrides which, when).
5. Produce a synthesized answer that incorporates all valid domain perspectives.

**The precedence rules** (rough — for the full rules, see `multi_mind_synthesis.md` and the ethics layer's red-lines file):
- 14_ETHICS overrides everything when a red line is involved. A decision that crosses a religious red line is not permissible, regardless of business or strategic benefit.
- 10_PERSONAL/spiritual overrides 09_BUSINESS when the conflict is between spiritual harm and business benefit. Riba is haram regardless of the IRR.
- 07_MINDS/sima_yi overrides 07_MINDS/lu_bu when the decision is long-horizon. Patience wins; force is for tactical windows.
- 12_DECISION/blind_spot_checker overrides everything before delivery. A decision that fails the blind spot check is not delivered, regardless of which layer approved it.

### The Synthesis Worked Example

**Situation**: A client offers the contractor a 3-month project at 25% above his normal rate, but the project is for a developer known to delay payments by 6-12 months, and the developer wants the contractor to take a 10% advance (instead of the standard 25%).

**Domain routes**:
- 09_BUSINESS/contracting/cash_flow_zero_capital: the cash flow math requires 25% advance to bridge materials and labor. At 10% advance, the contractor is in deficit from month 2. The business answer: decline unless the rate premium compensates for the cash cost of waiting.
- 09_BUSINESS/contracting/client_psychology_egypt: the developer's late-payment history indicates either cash flow trouble or exploitative practice. The business psychology answer: high risk of late payment; the rate premium may not be paid on time either.
- 10_PERSONAL/spiritual/rizq_and_tawakkul: rizq is from Allah, but the contractor is not excused from due diligence. The spiritual answer: do not act recklessly on the assumption that "rizq will come"; act prudently, and trust Allah with the result.
- 14_ETHICS/long_term_reputation: taking the project at high rate and then complaining publicly about late payment damages the contractor's reputation. The ethics answer: take only if you can absorb the late payment without complaint, or decline.
- 07_MINDS/sima_yi: patience — decline this project, preserve the working capital for better opportunities. The mind answer: decline.
- 07_MINDS/guo_jia: rapid exploitation — the 25% premium is an opportunity; capture it with contractual protections (penalty for late payment, shorter milestone gaps, supplier credit). The mind answer: accept with protections.
- 12_DECISION/blind_spot_checker: check for availability bias (recent good experience with another developer distorting risk perception), check for overconfidence (the contractor's belief that he can manage the late payment better than others).

**The synthesis**: The conflict is between Sima Yi (decline) and Guo Jia (accept with protections). The blind spot check is run. The business cash flow route says the protections must include at least 25% advance (or equivalent milestone structure). The ethics route says no public complaint if late payment occurs — only contractual mechanisms. The personal route says the contractor's bandwidth for late payment stress is limited; only one such project at a time.

The synthesized answer: accept with three conditions — (1) 25% advance (or equivalent milestone structure with shorter intervals); (2) contractual late-payment penalty at 1% per month; (3) the contractor can take no other high-risk project concurrently. If the developer accepts all three, take it. If the developer refuses any, decline.

This is what multi-domain routing produces: a synthesized answer that incorporates the business cash flow analysis, the client psychology read, the spiritual framework, the ethical constraint, the strategic mind tension, and the blind spot check. No single domain could have produced it.

## The Routing Discipline — Don't Route to the Wrong Layer

The most common routing failure is sending the decision to the wrong layer. Examples:
- Sending a religious question to 09_BUSINESS (when it should go to 14_ETHICS + 10_PERSONAL/spiritual). The contractor asks "is this contract halal?" and gets a business answer about IRR. Wrong route. The religious answer is in 14_ETHICS and 10_PERSONAL/spiritual.
- Sending a strategic question to 09_BUSINESS (when it should go to 07_MINDS + 08_STRATEGY). The contractor asks "should I scale from electrical to general contracting?" and gets a business answer about capacity. Wrong route. The strategic answer is in 07_MINDS (which mind?) and 08_STRATEGY (which doctrine?).
- Sending an ethical question to 12_DECISION (when it should go to 14_ETHICS). The contractor asks "is it permissible to use this tactic in negotiation?" and gets a decision-process answer. Wrong route. The ethical answer is in 14_ETHICS.

The discipline: ask, for each decision, "which layer's files would I read first if I had to answer this from one layer?" That layer is the primary route. Then ask, "which other layers would change the answer if I consulted them?" Those are the secondary routes. The synthesis is across all relevant routes.

**The test**: if your route produces an answer that ignores an obviously relevant consideration (e.g., the religious dimension of a contract; the strategic dimension of a hiring decision; the personal bandwidth dimension of a project decision), you routed wrong. Re-route.

## Common Pitfalls

**Pitfall 1: Single-domain routing.** Routing a decision to only one layer produces a partial answer. Most decisions are multi-domain. Force yourself to list at least three domains for any consequential decision.

**Pitfall 2: Routing ethics late.** Ethics should be the second route, not the last. If you do all the business and strategic analysis and only then check ethics, you've biased yourself toward the answer you've built. Check ethics early; let it constrain the analysis from the start.

**Pitfall 3: Treating the precedence rules as rigid.** The precedence (ethics > spiritual > long mind > blind spot) is a default, not an absolute. Sometimes a business consideration overrides a spiritual one (e.g., when the spiritual "tawakkul" is being used as cover for laziness — the business reality is that tawakkul requires effort, not passivity). The precedence is the starting point, not the end.

**Pitfall 4: Confusing routing with synthesis.** Routing identifies the layers; synthesis combines their outputs. A routed decision that lists each layer's answer separately, without integrating them, has not been synthesized. The integration is the work.

**Pitfall 5: Routing to the wrong layer.** The most common failure mode. If your route ignores an obvious consideration, re-route. The test: would a thoughtful outsider, looking at your answer, immediately notice a missing consideration? If yes, you routed wrong.
