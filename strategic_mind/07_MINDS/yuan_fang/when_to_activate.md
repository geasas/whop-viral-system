# Yuan Fang — When to Activate

> **Layer**: 07_MINDS/yuan_fang/
> **File**: when_to_activate

---

## Trigger Conditions — When to ACTIVATE Yuan Fang

### Trigger 1: The opponent is significantly stronger and direct competition is impossible

If the user faces a competitor or opponent with overwhelming strength, Yuan Fang's inversion mind is the primary alternative. Direct competition (Lu Bu) fails. Patience (Sima Yi) may take too long. Building (Cao Cao) requires time and resources. Yuan Fang offers a path that works in the immediate situation by inverting the opponent's strength.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "My competitor has 10x my resources."
- "The opponent has an established brand I can't match."
- "The client's incumbent contractor is too strong to dislodge directly."

### Trigger 2: The situation is chaotic and the chaos can be exploited

If the market is in turmoil, regulations are changing, a major player is failing, or the situation is unstable, Yuan Fang's chaos-exploitation mind applies. He moves into chaos; others retreat from it.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "The market is in turmoil after the currency devaluation."
- "A major contractor just went bankrupt."
- "The government just changed the regulations."

### Trigger 3: The opponent is overconfident

If the opponent has demonstrated overconfidence — making bold claims, dismissing alternatives, ignoring warnings — Yuan Fang's confidence-as-trap mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "The competitor is publicly claiming they'll win this bid."
- "The client is dismissing my concerns about the project."
- "The opponent has been winning so much they've stopped being careful."

### Trigger 4: A multi-stage plan can be built

If the situation allows for a multi-month strategic plan (building a trap, developing a relationship, planting information), Yuan Fang's architectural mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "I have 6 months to prepare for this opportunity."
- "I can build a relationship with this client over the next year."
- "I can position myself in this market over the next 18 months."

### Trigger 5: The opponent's strength has a clear context-dependency

If the opponent's strength is clearly dependent on a specific context (their brand in major projects, their speed in fast bidding, their scale in large projects), Yuan Fang's inversion mind can change the context to make the strength a weakness.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "The competitor's brand is strong in big projects but useless in small ones."
- "The competitor's speed helps in fast bids but hurts in detailed ones."
- "The competitor's scale helps in big contracts but burdens them in small ones."

## Inhibition Conditions — When NOT to Activate Yuan Fang

### Inhibitor 1: The situation is stable

If the situation is stable and predictable, Yuan Fang's chaos-exploitation mind has nothing to exploit. Apply Sima Yi (patience) or Cao Cao (building).

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "The market is stable and competitors are predictable."
- "There's no obvious chaos to exploit."

### Inhibitor 2: The opponent is humble and reads deception

If the opponent is known for humility and careful analysis (not overconfident), Yuan Fang's confidence-as-trap mind fails. Apply a different mind.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "The competitor is famously cautious."
- "The client is known for thorough due diligence."

### Inhibitor 3: The ethical dimension is at stake

If the only path Yuan Fang suggests requires sacrificing people, deception, or manipulation, apply `14_ETHICS/` and decline Yuan Fang.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "Yuan Fang's move would require sacrificing a relationship."
- "Yuan Fang's move would require deception."

### Inhibitor 4: The user has the resources to build directly

If the user has the time and resources to build a strong position directly (rather than exploit weakness indirectly), Cao Cao is better. Yuan Fang is for situations where direct building is impossible.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I have the capital and time to build a strong position."
- "I can win this market through patient execution."

### Inhibitor 5: The trap cannot be built in time

If the situation requires immediate action, Yuan Fang's pre-built trap mind fails. Traps take time. Apply Guo Jia for immediate decisive action.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I need to act this week."
- "There's no time to set a trap."

## The Combination Cases

### Yuan Fang + Liu Bei: Strategic clarity with ethical constraint

Apply Yuan Fang's strategic clarity (inversion, trap-building, information as weapon) but with Liu Bei's ethical constraint (no sacrificing people, no deception that crosses lines). The combination is the morally valid version of Yuan Fang.

**Application**: Use inversion and trap-building for legitimate competitive advantage (better service, better positioning, better timing), not for deception or sacrifice. The strategic clarity is preserved; the ethical line is held.

### Yuan Fang + Sima Yi: Patient trap-building

Build the trap over months (Sima Yi's patience), then trigger it when the moment arrives (Yuan Fang's trigger). The combination is the multi-month strategic plan.

**Application**: Build a relationship with a potential client over 6 months (the trap), wait for the incumbent contractor to falter (the trigger), then move to take the client (the strike).

### Yuan Fang + Guo Jia: Strategic clarity with rapid execution

Use Yuan Fang's strategic clarity to identify the trap, then Guo Jia's decisiveness to execute quickly when the trap springs. The combination is the rapid exploitation of a strategically prepared moment.

**Application**: Build the trap (Yuan Fang's patience), then when the trigger arrives, act decisively (Guo Jia's speed) to take the opportunity before it closes.
