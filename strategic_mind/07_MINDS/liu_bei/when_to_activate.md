# Liu Bei — When to Activate

## Trigger Conditions — When to ACTIVATE Liu Bei

### Trigger 1: The decision involves a long-term relationship

If the user is making a decision that affects a long-term relationship (partner, senior subcontractor, major client), Liu Bei is the primary mind.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "Should I take on this partner?"
- "Should I make this person a senior subcontractor?"
- "How should I handle this conflict with a long-term client?"

### Trigger 2: Reputation and trust are at stake

If the decision affects the user's reputation for integrity, Liu Bei's mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "Should I do the right thing even if it costs me?"
- "Will this decision damage my reputation if discovered?"
- "I'm tempted to take a shortcut, but I'm hesitating."

### Trigger 3: A relationship needs repair

If a relationship has been damaged and the user wants to repair it, Liu Bei's relational mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "I had a conflict with my subcontractor and want to fix it."
- "My partner feels I haven't been fair."
- "My client lost trust in me after a project delay."

### Trigger 4: The temptation to betray is strong

If the user is being tempted to betray for short-term gain, Liu Bei's mind applies as a counterweight.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "I could poach this client from my competitor, but it feels wrong."
- "I could underpay this subcontractor, but I'd lose his trust."
- "I could take this opportunity by deception, but..."

### Trigger 5: The mission and values of the business are being tested

If the decision involves the mission and values — whether the business stands for something beyond profit — Liu Bei's mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "Does this project align with my mission?"
- "Should I refuse this work because it doesn't fit my values?"
- "I'm being asked to do something that contradicts what I say I stand for."

## Inhibition Conditions — When NOT to Activate Liu Bei

### Inhibitor 1: The situation is purely tactical

If the decision is a single, short-term tactical choice (a bid, a single contract term), apply the appropriate tactical mind (Guo Jia for speed, Sima Yi for patience, Lu Bu for directness).

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "Should I accept this specific contract term?"
- "Should I bid on this single project?"

### Inhibitor 2: The situation requires rapid action

If the window is closing fast, Liu Bei's slower relational approach may be too slow. Apply Guo Jia.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I need to decide in 24 hours."
- "The window closes tomorrow."

### Inhibitor 3: The opponent is ruthless and emotional appeals will fail

If the opponent has demonstrated they don't respond to relational appeals (they're transactional or predatory), Liu Bei's warmth is wasted. Apply Sima Yi's cold calculation.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "This client has a reputation for being ruthless."
- "This partner has betrayed others before."

### Inhibitor 4: The decision requires building systems, not relationships

If the decision is about building systems (training, processes, certifications), Liu Bei's relational mind is less applicable. Apply Cao Cao.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I'm building a training program for my team."
- "I'm setting up the company's accounting system."
- "I'm implementing quality control standards."

### Inhibitor 5: The situation requires suspicion

If the situation has clear signs of deception or betrayal, applying Liu Bei's trust would be naive. Apply Sima Yi's selective suspicion.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I've been told this person is plotting against me."
- "The signs of betrayal are clear."

---

## The Combination Cases

### Liu Bei + Cao Cao: Heart and structure

Build the systems (Cao Cao) while building the relationships (Liu Bei). The combination is the most durable form of business.

**Application**: Build the certifications, the training, the systems, AND build the personal relationships with key people. The systems carry the day-to-day; the relationships carry the crisis.

### Liu Bei + Sima Yi: Trust with verification

Trust as default (Liu Bei), but verify (Sima Yi). The verification catches betrayal early; the trust creates the conditions for loyalty.

**Application**: Trust subcontractors but verify their work. Trust clients but verify their payment capacity. The verification is not suspicion; it's good business.

### Liu Bei + Guo Jia: Warmth and decisiveness

Make decisions quickly (Guo Jia) but communicate them with warmth (Liu Bei). The combination preserves relationships even during rapid action.

**Application**: When you must decline a project, decline quickly but with care. Don't leave the client hanging; don't reject them coldly.
