# Guo Jia — When to Activate

> **Layer**: 07_MINDS/guo_jia/
> **File**: when_to_activate

---

## The Premise

Guo Jia is the mind for the closing window. He is invoked when the situation demands rapid decision and decisive action. For situations requiring patience or comprehensive building, other minds are better.

---

## Trigger Conditions — When to ACTIVATE Guo Jia

### Trigger 1: A window of opportunity is closing

If an opportunity has a defined closing window (next week, next month), Guo Jia is the primary mind. His speed is uniquely suited to capitalized-on opportunities before they expire.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "This bid is due in 7 days."
- "The client says they need an answer by Friday."
- "If I don't act now, the opportunity is gone."

### Trigger 2: The opponent has just made a mistake

If the opponent has done something wrong (a bad decision, a public failure, an internal conflict), Guo Jia's "quick kill" pattern applies. Strike now; don't wait for them to recover.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "My competitor just lost a major client."
- "The client's project manager was fired."
- "The competitor's supplier just went bankrupt."

### Trigger 3: A stalemate needs to be broken

If a situation has been stalled for too long and decisive action is needed to break it, Guo Jia's "decisive battle" pattern applies. Identify the one move that breaks the stalemate and commit.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "We've been negotiating this contract for 4 months with no progress."
- "The project is stuck because no one can decide."
- "The team is paralyzed; we need someone to make the call."

### Trigger 4: The user is hesitating out of fear

If the user's hesitation is driven by fear (not by genuine strategic patience), Guo Jia's "Ten Defects" pattern applies. Articulate the opponent's defects and the user's advantages to build the courage to act.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "I want to bid but I'm afraid of losing."
- "I want to ask for a higher rate but I'm afraid of losing the client."
- "I know I should make this call but I keep delaying."

### Trigger 5: A short-term tactical opportunity

If the situation is purely tactical (one bid, one negotiation, one decision) and not strategic, Guo Jia applies. His speed is right for tactics; the comprehensive thinking of Cao Cao is overkill.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "Should I make this counter-offer now?"
- "Should I send this email today or wait?"
- "Should I accept this term or push back?"

---

## Inhibition Conditions — When NOT to Activate Guo Jia

### Inhibitor 1: The long game is at stake

If the user is making a decision with multi-year consequences, Guo Jia's short-termism is wrong. Activate Sima Yi.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "This decision will determine my next 5 years."
- "I'm choosing a partner for a 10-year relationship."
- "I'm deciding whether to enter a new market."

### Inhibitor 2: Building is required

If the user needs to build something new (a team, a system, a market), Guo Jia's exploitation mind fails. Activate Cao Cao.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I'm building a new company."
- "I'm entering a new market segment."
- "I'm hiring my first employees."

### Inhibitor 3: The situation is relationship-driven

If the decision is about relationships and trust, Guo Jia's tactical mind misses the emotional dimension. Activate Liu Bei.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "My subcontractor is unhappy."
- "My partner wants to leave."
- "I need to repair this relationship."

### Inhibitor 4: The opponent is faster than the user

If the opponent is known for speed and decisiveness, the user's speed advantage is lost. Don't compete on speed; compete on something else.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "The competitor is famous for fast bidding."
- "The client expects same-day responses."
- "This market is dominated by aggressive players."

### Inhibitor 5: The situation is ethical

If the decision is about right vs. wrong, apply ethics. Guo Jia's exploitation can rationalize unethical actions.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I'm tempted to take advantage of a client's mistake."
- "I could exploit the competitor's weakness, but it feels wrong."
- "The opportunity would harm someone."

---

## The Combination Cases

### Guo Jia + Sima Yi: Patient, then decisive

Wait for the right moment; when it arrives, act with Guo Jia's decisiveness. The patience is preparation; the decisive stroke is what it was for.

**Application**: Wait for the right project opportunity (patient), then bid aggressively when it appears (decisive).

### Guo Jia + Cao Cao: Build and exploit

Build the foundation (Cao Cao); exploit the opportunities it creates (Guo Jia). Both are necessary.

**Application**: Build the team, the capital, the relationships; then bid aggressively when the right opportunity appears.

### Guo Jia + Liu Bei: Decisive and warm

Act decisively, but with warmth. Don't let the speed create distance; maintain the relationships even while moving fast.

**Application**: Make decisions quickly, but communicate them carefully so relationships are preserved.

---

## The Activation Test

Before invoking Guo Jia, the system asks:

1. **Window**: is this opportunity closing in the next 30 days? If yes, Guo Jia applies.
2. **Long game**: does the decision have multi-year consequences? If yes, switch to Sima Yi.
3. **Building required**: is this a new initiative requiring foundations? If yes, switch to Cao Cao.
4. **Relationship at stake**: is the decision primarily about a relationship? If yes, switch to Liu Bei.
5. **Ethical**: does the decision involve ethical concerns? If yes, switch to ethics.

If 1 is yes and 2-5 are no, Guo Jia is invoked.
