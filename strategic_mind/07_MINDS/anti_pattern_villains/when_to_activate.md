# Anti-Pattern Villains — When to Activate (The Periodic Audit Format)

> **Layer**: 07_MINDS/anti_pattern_villains/
> **File**: when_to_activate
> **Subject**: The explicit routing rules for when to invoke the anti-pattern villains mind AS A PERIODIC AUDIT — and the form of the audit, the specific diagnostic questions for each of the five diseases, and the routing to the counter-mind for each
> **Character class**: COLLECTIVE ANTI-PATTERN — invoke as a periodic AUDIT, never as a template. The individual anti-pattern minds (yuan_shao, yuan_shu, dong_zhuo, lu_bu, liu_biao, liu_zhang) have their own `when_to_activate.md` files for the specific trigger conditions of each villain. This file provides the cross-cutting audit format that runs across all five diseases.

---

## The Premise

The anti-pattern villains mind is invoked differently from every other mind in this system. The strategic minds are invoked when the user needs to think a certain way. The anti-pattern villains mind is invoked when the system detects that the user is thinking a certain way — and needs to stop — OR when the user proactively requests a periodic self-audit.

The anti-pattern villains mind is the diagnostic meta-mind. The system runs the user's situation through the five diseases (Premature Action, Vanity Without Substance, Indecision in Crisis, Betrayal as Pattern, Civil War After Tyranny) and asks: is the user exhibiting any of these patterns? If yes, the system warns the user, identifies which specific anti-pattern villain exemplifies the pattern most strongly, and routes the user to the appropriate counter-mind.

The anti-pattern villains mind is never invoked to give strategic advice. It is invoked to give a warning. The advice that follows the warning comes from another mind. The anti-pattern villains file is the diagnostic; the counter-mind is the prescription.

This file defines (1) the periodic audit schedule, (2) the form of the audit, (3) the specific diagnostic questions for each of the five diseases, (4) the routing rules for which counter-mind to invoke after each disease is detected, and (5) the combination cases where multiple diseases are active simultaneously.

---

## The Periodic Audit Schedule

The anti-pattern villains audit should be run on three schedules:

### Schedule 1: Monthly Audit

On the first of every month, the system should run the full anti-pattern audit. The full audit asks all five diagnostic blocks (one for each disease). The monthly audit is the maintenance audit — it catches patterns that have crept in over the previous month and that the user has not noticed. The monthly audit takes 15-30 minutes if the user answers honestly. The discipline of running the audit monthly is the discipline of not letting the patterns compound for more than a month before they are caught.

### Schedule 2: Major Decision Audit

Before every major decision (a project bid above a threshold size, a key hire, a key fire, a partnership formation, a partnership dissolution, a real estate acquisition, a major capital expenditure), the system should run the relevant block of the anti-pattern audit. The relevant block is the block for the disease most likely to be triggered by the decision type:

- Bid / acquisition / expansion decision → Premature Action block (Pillar 1)
- Credential / award / contract receipt → Vanity Without Substance block (Pillar 2)
- Crisis decision / cash flow / client loss → Indecision in Crisis block (Pillar 3)
- Alliance switch / subcontractor change / supplier change → Betrayal as Pattern block (Pillar 4)
- Partner removal / business restructuring → Civil War After Tyranny block (Pillar 5)

The major decision audit is a focused audit. It does not run all five blocks. It runs the block most relevant to the decision. The focused audit takes 5-10 minutes. The discipline of running the focused audit before every major decision is the discipline of not making the decision in the anti-pattern mode that the decision type tends to trigger.

### Schedule 3: Quarterly Comprehensive Audit

Once per quarter (the first of January, April, July, October), the system should run the comprehensive anti-pattern audit. The comprehensive audit runs all five blocks AND the cross-cutting blind spot audit (from blind_spots.md). The comprehensive audit takes 60-90 minutes if the user answers honestly. The discipline of running the comprehensive audit quarterly is the discipline of catching patterns that have compounded over the quarter and that the monthly audits may have missed.

The quarterly audit should be accompanied by a written record — the user's answers to each question, the patterns detected, the counter-minds invoked, the actions committed to in the next 30 days. The written record is the user's accountability document. The document should be reviewed at the next quarterly audit. The review is the discipline of seeing whether the patterns have been corrected or have compounded.

## The Form of the Audit

When the system invokes the anti-pattern audit, the audit should take a specific form. The form is:

1. **Name the disease**: "We will audit you for [disease name]."
2. **Explain the disease briefly**: "[Disease name] is the pattern of [one-sentence summary]. The villain most associated with it is [villain name]. The mechanism is [one-sentence mechanism]."
3. **Ask the diagnostic questions**: Ask each diagnostic question for the disease. The user answers honestly. The system does not interpret the answers; the system records the answers.
4. **Score the disease**: For each "yes" answer, score one point. The score is the disease's intensity for the user in this audit cycle.
5. **If the score is 2 or more (out of 5), invoke the warning**: The warning names the pattern, cites the historical example, maps to the user's situation, states the predicted consequence, and recommends the counter-move.
6. **Route to the counter-mind**: The counter-mind is the specific mind (Sima Yi, Cao Cao, Guo Jia, Liu Bei, Zhuge Liang, Jia Xu, Sun Quan) that provides the cure for the disease. The user is asked to consult the relevant file in the system and to apply the counter-move in the next 30 days.
7. **Record the audit**: The audit, the scores, the warnings, and the counter-moves are recorded in the user's audit log. The log is the user's accountability document. The log is reviewed at the next quarterly audit.

The form is direct. It does not flatter the user. It does not soften the audit. The anti-pattern patterns are, by definition, patterns the user is falling into because the easier path is seductive. The audit must be sharp enough to break the seduction. Flattery would be Yuan Shao's own pattern (counsel that feels welcome). The audit must be counsel that feels unwelcome — and is correct.

## The Diagnostic Questions — The 5 Disease Blocks

### Block 1: Premature Action (Pillar 1)

The disease of premature action is the disease of the man who acts before his base is ready. The villain most associated with it is Yuan Shu (the imperial declaration in 197 AD) and the Yellow Turbans (the 184 AD uprising).

**Diagnostic questions**:

1. Are you considering an action (a project bid, a business expansion, a real estate acquisition, a major hire) that would skip a step on your path? (Yes / No)
2. Is the action you are considering larger than any you have previously executed, by a factor of 2x or more? (Yes / No)
3. Do you have the crew, the bonding, the systems, and the cash reserves to execute the action if it succeeds? (Yes / No) — Note: a "No" here is the diagnostic signal.
4. Are you treating the singularity of the moment as evidence that the action must be taken now, rather than as evidence that the action is a trap? (Yes / No)
5. Have you considered whether the path you are skipping is the substance the destination requires — and whether skipping the path destroys the substance? (Yes / No) — Note: a "No" here is the diagnostic signal.

**Scoring**: Count the "Yes" answers to questions 1, 2, 4, and the "No" answers to questions 3 and 5 (the "wrong-direction" answers). A score of 2 or more indicates the disease is active.

**Warning form**: "You are about to make [Yuan Shu's / the Yellow Turbans'] premature-action mistake. [Historical example]. Your situation is similar because [specific parallel]. If you proceed, the likely result is [specific consequence in the user's domain]."

**Counter-mind routing**:
- Score 2 → Sima Yi (patient counterpunching — wait for the base, refuse the false opportunity)
- Score 3 → Cao Cao (institutional building — build the substance, let the symbol follow)
- Score 4-5 → Sima Yi + Cao Cao (the base must be built patiently AND the substance must be institutionalized; both counter-minds are required)

---

### Block 2: Vanity Without Substance (Pillar 2)

The disease of vanity without substance is the disease of the man who has a name, a credential, a symbol, a single major success — and concludes that the symbol is the substance. The villain most associated with it is Yuan Shao (the prestige filter) and Yuan Shu (the seal as the mandate).

**Diagnostic questions**:

1. Have you recently obtained a credential, an award, a certification, or a major contract that you are treating as evidence of a new class of capability? (Yes / No)
2. When you receive counsel, do you find yourself more receptive to the advisor who agrees with your instinct than to the advisor who challenges it? (Yes / No)
3. Are you evaluating counsel partly by the standing of the counselor (his title, his lineage, his prior successes, his relationship to you) rather than entirely by the content of the counsel? (Yes / No)
4. When you imagine making a decision, do you first imagine how it will look to others — and choose the version of the decision that photographs better? (Yes / No)
5. Have you stopped building the crews, the systems, the cash reserves, or the client relationships because you believe the recent credential (or award, or contract) has done the work? (Yes / No)

**Scoring**: Count the "Yes" answers. A score of 2 or more indicates the disease is active.

**Warning form**: "You are about to make [Yuan Shao's / Yuan Shu's] vanity-without-substance mistake. [Historical example]. Your situation is similar because [specific parallel]. If you proceed, the likely result is [specific consequence in the user's domain]."

**Counter-mind routing**:
- Score 2 → Guo Jia (cold reading — evaluate the situation, not your self-image)
- Score 3 → Cao Cao (substance over symbol — build the capability, let the symbol follow)
- Score 4-5 → Sima Yi (concealment — do not display the symbol; display only the substance when the display serves) + Liu Bei (relational trust — build relationships where hard counsel is welcome)

---

### Block 3: Indecision in Crisis (Pillar 3)

The disease of indecision in crisis is the disease of the man who has a position to defend and cannot execute the defense move on time. The villain most associated with it is He Jin (the 189 AD hesitation) and Yuan Shao (the Guandu delay).

**Diagnostic questions**:

1. Are you currently facing a decision with a closing window (a bid deadline, a client ultimatum, a supplier demand, a cash flow cliff) — and have you been deliberating for more than a week without action? (Yes / No)
2. Are you convening a council of advisors (your partners, your foreman, your accountant, your lawyer, your family) and waiting for the council to agree before you can act? (Yes / No)
3. Are you describing your refusal to commit as "keeping options open" — when the options are decaying with each week of delay? (Yes / No)
4. Are you delaying a decision because the decision will displease someone you care about (a partner, a family member, a senior employee, a long-time client)? (Yes / No)
5. Have you, in the last 30 days, missed a window or made a late decision that produced a worse outcome than an on-time decision would have? (Yes / No)

**Scoring**: Count the "Yes" answers. A score of 2 or more indicates the disease is active.

**Warning form**: "You are about to make [He Jin's / Yuan Shao's / Liu Zhang's] indecision-in-crisis mistake. [Historical example]. Your situation is similar because [specific parallel]. If you proceed, the likely result is [specific consequence in the user's domain]."

**Counter-mind routing**:
- Score 2 → Guo Jia (rapid exploitation — decide with what you have)
- Score 3 → Cao Cao (decisive synthesis — hear the views, integrate, decide alone, act)
- Score 4-5 → Guo Jia + Cao Cao (decide immediately with available information, then execute decisively; the indecision has cost you the perfect decision, but the on-time imperfect decision beats the late perfect decision)

---

### Block 4: Betrayal as Pattern (Pillar 4)

The disease of betrayal as pattern is the disease of the man who treats alliance as a temporary arrangement and who switches sides whenever the switch serves him. The villain most associated with it is Lu Bu (the canonical case) and Li Jue/Guo Si (the post-Dong Zhuo civil war).

**Diagnostic questions**:

1. Have you, in the last 12 months, switched a major relationship (a subcontractor, a supplier, a client, a partner) for short-term gain without giving the relationship a chance to be repaired? (Yes / No)
2. Are you currently contemplating switching a major relationship for short-term gain (a slightly cheaper subcontractor, a slightly faster supplier, a slightly larger client)? (Yes / No)
3. Are you evaluating alliances purely on the instrumental axis (does this alliance serve me, today?) — without considering the relational axis (does this alliance build the network that will serve me, in the next crisis)? (Yes / No)
4. Have you broken an alliance in a way that the other party would describe as a betrayal — even if you would describe it as a rational cost-saving? (Yes / No)
5. Is your network of subcontractors, suppliers, and clients narrowing year by year — and do you not understand why? (Yes / No)

**Scoring**: Count the "Yes" answers. A score of 2 or more indicates the disease is active.

**Warning form**: "You are about to make Lu Bu's betrayal-as-pattern mistake. [Historical example]. Your situation is similar because [specific parallel]. If you proceed, the likely result is [specific consequence in the user's domain]."

**Counter-mind routing**:
- Score 2 → Liu Bei (loyalty as the foundation — the alliance is the asset, not the cost to be optimized)
- Score 3 → Sima Yi (patient alliance — the alliance that lasts decades produces more than the alliance that lasts months)
- Score 4-5 → Liu Bei + Sima Yi (the alliance must be maintained as a long-term asset; the network that has narrowed must be rebuilt through demonstrated loyalty over years; both counter-minds are required)

---

### Block 5: Civil War After Tyranny (Pillar 5)

The disease of civil war after tyranny is the disease of the man who removes (or is about to remove) a tyrant (a partner, a senior employee, a difficult client, a structural element of the business) without preparing the successor structure. The villain most associated with it is Wang Yun (the post-Dong Zhuo chaos) and Yuan Shao's sons (the post-Yuan Shao civil war).

**Diagnostic questions**:

1. Are you contemplating the removal of a partner (a brother, a cousin, an investor, a senior employee) — without a documented succession plan, a documented transition, and a prepared structure for the post-removal period? (Yes / No)
2. Are you treating the removal as the liberation — and assuming the structure will sort itself out after the removal? (Yes / No)
3. Have you named a successor in your business? Have you documented your processes so someone else could run the business? Have you given your key employees the authority they would need to run things if you were not there? (Yes / No to all three) — Note: a "No" to any of the three is the diagnostic signal.
4. Are you avoiding the succession decision because the decision will displease someone you care about? (Yes / No)
5. If you were unable to run the business tomorrow (illness, accident, death), would the business continue to operate — or would it collapse? (Continue / Collapse) — Note: "Collapse" is the diagnostic signal.

**Scoring**: Count the "Yes" answers to questions 1, 2, 4, and the "wrong-direction" answers to questions 3 and 5. A score of 2 or more indicates the disease is active.

**Warning form**: "You are about to make [Wang Yun's / Yuan Shao's] civil-war-after-tyranny mistake. [Historical example]. Your situation is similar because [specific parallel]. If you proceed, the likely result is [specific consequence in the user's domain]."

**Counter-mind routing**:
- Score 2 → Cao Cao (institutional building — the structure that survives the leader is the leader's true legacy)
- Score 3 → Sima Yi (patient succession — the order is built over years, not by a single act of removal)
- Score 4-5 → Cao Cao + Sima Yi + Liu Bei (the structure must be built institutionally, the order must be prepared patiently over years, and the successor must be a person whose loyalty has been tested; all three counter-minds are required)

## The Cross-Cutting Blind Spot Audit (Quarterly)

In addition to the five disease blocks, the quarterly audit should run the cross-cutting blind spot audit. The blind spot audit asks, for each of the six blind spots (from blind_spots.md):

1. **Advantages becoming liabilities**: Are you treating your advantages (your trade, your network, your capital) as proof of your rightness — rather than as tools to be used carefully?
2. **Indecision as decision**: Are you treating your failure to decide as the absence of decision — rather than as a decision to let circumstances choose?
3. **Symbol as substance**: Are you treating a recent credential, award, or contract as evidence of a new class of capability — rather than as a checkpoint on the path of building?
4. **Rejecting the saving counsel**: Are you rejecting counsel that feels unwelcome — when the unwelcome counsel is the counsel that would save you?
5. **Reputation compounding**: Are you treating each alliance as a short-term cost to be optimized — when the alliance is a compounding asset?
6. **Removal without structure**: Are you contemplating the removal of a partner without preparing the successor structure — when the removal without the preparation is the civil war?

For each blind spot, the answer is "yes," "no," or "unsure." The "yes" answers trigger the corresponding counter-mind (Sima Yi, Guo Jia, Cao Cao, Liu Bei, Cao Cao again, Cao Cao again). The "unsure" answers trigger the deeper individual anti-pattern file (yuan_shao/blind_spots.md, yuan_shu/blind_spots.md, dong_zhuo/blind_spots.md, lu_bu/blind_spots.md, liu_biao/blind_spots.md, liu_zhang/blind_spots.md) for the specific diagnostic that the user is unsure about.

## Inhibition Conditions — When NOT to Activate the Anti-Pattern Audit

There are situations where the anti-pattern audit would be inappropriate. The system should not run the audit when:

### Inhibitor 1: The user is in a genuine strategic-flow state

If the user is in the middle of executing a strategy (in a flow state, mid-decision, mid-project), the audit would interrupt the flow. The audit should be scheduled for the beginning or end of the flow, not the middle. The discipline of running the audit at the right time is the discipline of not letting the audit become a form of indecision in crisis.

### Inhibitor 2: The user has recently completed a corrective cycle

If the user has, in the last 30 days, completed a corrective cycle (detected an anti-pattern, applied the counter-mind, executed the counter-move), the audit should be lighter — focusing on whether the correction has held, not on whether new patterns have emerged. The lighter audit is the recognition that the user has done the work and that the work should be acknowledged.

### Inhibitor 3: The user is in a genuine emergency

If the user is in a genuine emergency (a cash flow cliff, a client crisis, a safety incident), the audit is the wrong tool. The emergency requires the strategic mind (Cao Cao for decisive action, Guo Jia for rapid exploitation). The audit is for the periods between emergencies, when the patterns creep in. The audit during an emergency would be a form of indecision in crisis — which is itself one of the diseases the audit is meant to detect.

## The Combination Cases

The anti-pattern villains mind is a diagnostic meta-mind, not a strategic mind. The mind is never combined with other minds in the way that Sima Yi and Guo Jia are combined (patient then decisive). The anti-pattern villains mind is invoked alone as a periodic audit, and then a counter-mind is invoked as the corrective. The combinations are:

### Anti-pattern audit → Sima Yi corrective
For: premature action (Pillar 1), advantages becoming liabilities (Blind Spot 1), image-optimized decisions where substance-optimized decisions would work better.

### Anti-pattern audit → Cao Cao corrective
For: vanity without substance (Pillar 2), indecision in crisis (Pillar 3) when decisive action is required, civil war after tyranny (Pillar 5).

### Anti-pattern audit → Guo Jia corrective
For: indecision in crisis (Pillar 3) when the window is closing, premature action (Pillar 1) when the decision must be made with imperfect information.

### Anti-pattern audit → Liu Bei corrective
For: betrayal as pattern (Pillar 4), rejecting the saving counsel (Blind Spot 4), reputation compounding (Blind Spot 5).

### Anti-pattern audit → Zhuge Liang corrective
For: premature action (Pillar 1) when the user needs vision, not action; for vanity without substance (Pillar 2) when the user needs vision-driven substance, not symbol-driven vanity.

### Anti-pattern audit → Jia Xu corrective
For: betrayal as pattern (Pillar 4) when the user needs cynical realism about survival, not loyalty as foundation; for civil war after tyranny (Pillar 5) when the user needs to calculate the survival outcome of the removal.

Each combination is a one-way flow: the anti-pattern audit diagnoses, the counter-mind corrects. The anti-pattern is never the destination; the anti-pattern is always the warning sign on the way to the destination.

## The Self-Correction

The anti-pattern villains mind, because it is a warning mind, does not need the kind of self-correction that the strategic minds need (e.g., Sima Yi's tendency to over-wait). The anti-pattern mind's self-correction is built into the audit: the audit asks whether the user is exhibiting anti-patterns, and if yes, recommends the counter-mind.

The discipline for the user is to take the audit seriously — to actually answer the questions honestly, and to actually act on the counter-mind recommendations. The discipline is the work of a lifetime, because the anti-pattern patterns are the default patterns of a successful person who has not examined his success. The default does not change once; it changes continuously, requiring continuous examination.

The contractor who audits himself against the anti-pattern patterns every month, and acts on what he finds, will not become one of the men in this catalog. The contractor who does not audit himself will become one of them — slowly, comfortably, and without noticing until it is too late.

## The Self-Diagnostic Question (The One-Question Test)

In addition to the full audit, the system should offer a one-question self-diagnostic that the user can run at any moment. The question is:

> **"Am I making [villain name]'s mistake right now?"**

The villain name is selected based on the user's current situation:

- If the user is about to declare a destination (register a larger business, lease a larger office, claim a larger title) → "Am I making Yuan Shu's mistake right now?"
- If the user is delaying a decision while his advisors argue → "Am I making Yuan Shao's mistake right now?"
- If the user is treating a credential as proof of a new class of capability → "Am I making Yuan Shu's mistake right now?"
- If the user is hesitating to execute a winning move → "Am I making He Jin's mistake right now?"
- If the user is considering switching a major relationship for short-term gain → "Am I making Lu Bu's mistake right now?"
- If the user is about to remove a partner without preparing the successor structure → "Am I making Wang Yun's mistake right now?"
- If the user is building a business that depends on his personal presence without a succession plan → "Am I making Yuan Shao's mistake right now?"
- If the user is holding a strategic territory without consolidating it → "Am I making Liu Biao's mistake right now?"
- If the user has inherited a system and is avoiding the decisions the inheritance requires → "Am I making Liu Zhang's mistake right now?"
- If the user is rising before his base is ready → "Am I making the Yellow Turbans' mistake right now?"

If the answer is "yes" or "maybe," the user should consult the corresponding individual anti-pattern file (yuan_shu/, yuan_shao/, dong_zhuo/, lu_bu/, liu_biao/, liu_zhang/) for the deep diagnostic, and then apply the counter-mind the file recommends.

The one-question test is the trigger for the deeper audit. The test is the user's first line of defense against the patterns. The discipline of running the test in moments of decision is the discipline of catching the patterns before they produce their consequences.

## The Final Note

The anti-pattern villains mind is the most important mind in this system for a successful user — because the anti-pattern patterns are the patterns the user is most likely to fall into without realizing it. The other minds are aspirations (Sima Yi's patience, Cao Cao's decisiveness, Liu Bei's integrity, Guo Jia's speed, Zhuge Liang's vision). The anti-pattern patterns are the destination the user is heading toward by default. The anti-pattern villains file is the road sign that says "you are going the wrong way." The road sign is consulted not to be followed but to be obeyed — by turning around.

The system's job is to invoke the anti-pattern audit at the right moments — the moments when the user is about to make an anti-pattern move, and the periodic moments when the user is most likely to have drifted into the patterns. The user's job is to take the audit seriously, to answer the questions honestly, to act on the counter-mind recommendations, and to record the audit cycle for accountability. The discipline is the work. The work is the price of not becoming one of the men in this catalog.

The men in this catalog had every advantage the contractor has, and more. They did not have the discipline of self-examination. The discipline of self-examination is what this file is for. The discipline is invoked periodically, not constantly. The periodicity is the discipline.
