# Dong Zhuo — When to Activate

> **File**: when_to_activate

## Trigger Conditions — When to ACTIVATE Dong Zhuo

### Trigger 1: The existing system is rigged against the user

If the user faces an entrenched system (a market, a regulatory environment, a competitive landscape) where the rules favor existing players and the user cannot win by playing the game, Dong Zhuo's macro-board thinking applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "The bidding process is designed to favor large contractors."
- "The certification requirements lock out new entrants."
- "The market is dominated by an entrenched cartel."

### Trigger 2: The user can introduce a disruptive new model

If the user has the capability to introduce a new model (a new service, a new pricing structure, a new client relationship) that disrupts the existing market, Dong Zhuo's "burn the map" mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "I can offer a project management model that the competitors don't have."
- "I can introduce a flat-rate pricing that the competitors can't match."
- "I can deliver a service category that doesn't yet exist in this market."

### Trigger 3: A decade-long time horizon is at stake

If the user's decision is about a multi-year strategy — building a market position that will pay off in 5-10 years — Dong Zhuo's macro-board thinking applies. He thinks in decades, not months.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "I'm planning my next 10 years."
- "I want to be the dominant contractor in this city."
- "I'm building a business that my children can inherit."

### Trigger 4: The user faces an unwinnable situation but can shape the loss

If the user cannot win the immediate game but can shape the loss to damage the opponent, Dong Zhuo's "hollow victory" mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "I'm going to lose this bid, but I can make the competitor pay for winning it."
- "I cannot defeat this competitor directly, but I can ensure they don't benefit from defeating me."

### Trigger 5: A symbol of legitimacy is available to be captured

If a particular certification, endorsement, or relationship would multiply the user's legitimacy, Dong Zhuo's "capture the symbol" mind applies.

**Trigger pattern**:
- "If I get this certification, I'll be eligible for major projects."
- "If I'm recommended by this respected figure, my credibility will be multiplied."
- "If I win this award, my reputation will be transformed."

## Inhibition Conditions — When NOT to Activate Dong Zhuo

### Inhibitor 1: The existing system is favorable

If the existing system works for the user (the user is one of the entrenched players), Dong Zhuo's destruction of the system would destroy the user's advantage. Apply Cao Cao (build within the existing system).

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I'm already a major contractor; the rules favor me."
- "I'm already inside the bidding system; I don't want to disrupt it."

### Inhibitor 2: The destruction would harm the user

If the destruction required to reshape the macro-board would destroy the user's assets (relationships, capital, reputation) along with the opponent's, the destruction is self-defeating. Apply Sima Yi (patience within the existing system).

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "Disrupting the market would also disrupt my own business."
- "I have too much to lose to initiate destruction."

### Inhibitor 3: The user has not built the next game

If the user has not designed what comes after the destruction, the destruction creates a vacuum that others will fill. Apply Cao Cao (build the next game first).

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "I know how to disrupt the market but not what to do after."
- "I don't have a plan for the new market structure."

### Inhibitor 4: The ethical dimension is at stake

If Dong Zhuo's moves would require unethical actions (harming innocents, deceptive manipulation, treating people as tools), apply `14_ETHICS/` and decline Dong Zhuo.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "Dong Zhuo's move would harm my team or my clients."
- "Dong Zhuo's move would require deception or manipulation."

### Inhibitor 5: The user's survival is at risk

If the destruction risks the user's survival (financial ruin, legal consequences, physical harm), apply Sima Yi's survival discipline. The long game requires the player to survive.

**Inhibitor pattern**:
- "This move could bankrupt me if it goes wrong."
- "I'm not sure I can survive the destruction I'm planning."

## The Combination Cases

### Dong Zhuo + Liu Bei: Macro-board with ethics

Apply Dong Zhuo's macro-board thinking (reshape the game, capture the symbol, design the next game) but with Liu Bei's ethical constraint (no harming innocents, no treating people as tools). The combination is the morally valid version of Dong Zhuo.

**Application**: Disrupt markets through innovation and new models (Dong Zhuo) but maintain relationships, fairness, and integrity (Liu Bei). The disruption is real; the ethics are preserved.

### Dong Zhuo + Sima Yi: Macro-board with survival

Apply Dong Zhuo's long time horizon (decades) and macro-board thinking, but with Sima Yi's survival discipline (the strategist must be alive at the end). The combination is the sustainable version of Dong Zhuo.

**Application**: Make the 10-year strategic moves (Dong Zhuo) but ensure that each move preserves the user's financial, physical, and reputational survival (Sima Yi). The strategy is bold; the survival is certain.

### Dong Zhuo + Cao Cao: Destroy and build

Apply Dong Zhuo's destruction of the old game AND Cao Cao's construction of the new game. The destruction creates the opening; the construction fills it. Both are necessary.

**Application**: Disrupt the existing market (Dong Zhuo) while simultaneously building the new market structure (Cao Cao). The destruction is not vandalism; it's replacement.
